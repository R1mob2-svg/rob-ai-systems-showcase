# Case Study: Entreprenuity Smart Website, Kerry, and Digital Ambush

## Business Problem

Small and service businesses often have disconnected websites, lead forms, advertising, follow-up, and customer communication. Entreprenuity's smart-website work explores how those pieces can be connected into one AI-assisted customer journey.

## Smart Website Architecture

The commercial web system combines a React/Vite frontend with serverless API routes and AI-assisted customer workflows. Work across the project has included:

- AI concierge behaviour through Kerry
- Prospect-aware conversation context
- Lead capture and follow-up concepts
- Serverless API routing
- Persistent visitor/chat context patterns
- Analytics and conversion instrumentation
- Secure public/private assistant boundaries
- Vercel deployment and preview workflows
- Responsive/mobile QA

## Kerry

Kerry is the customer-facing AI guide. The engineering problem is broader than putting a chat box on a page: Kerry must receive bounded business context, avoid leaking internal infrastructure, maintain useful conversation state, and route customer intent into appropriate business actions.

The project has included work around model-provider integration, queue/status patterns, context persistence, safe visual artifacts, and prospect-specific behaviour.

## Digital Ambush

Digital Ambush is a reusable prospect workflow designed to create a highly personalised first experience for a business prospect.

The pattern combines:

- Evidence-backed prospect research
- Business-specific homepage concepts
- Current-versus-proposed visual framing
- Trade-specific questions
- AI-guided explanation of design decisions
- Engagement telemetry
- Prospect-specific conversation context
- Revision-request capture
- Clear separation between verified evidence, illustrative scenarios, and proposed outcomes

A core rule is that the system must not fabricate rankings, traffic, revenue loss, testimonials, or performance gains simply to make the sales page sound stronger.

## My Role

I own the commercial concept, product requirements, system architecture, acceptance rules, customer journey, AI-agent direction, testing priorities, and final product decisions. AI coding agents are used heavily in implementation, with the output reviewed against the intended live behaviour.

## Why It Matters

This work demonstrates the connection between agentic AI engineering and an actual commercial workflow: research a business, create a relevant digital concept, explain it intelligently, capture interaction, handle requested revisions, and move the prospect toward a real human/business outcome.
