Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need planning permission for my extension?

Many rear extensions fall within Permitted Development rights. We assess your exact property boundaries and manage all planning or lawful development certificate submissions.

How do fixed-price stage payments work?

Payments are tied directly to agreed physical milestones: foundations, structural steel, weather-tight shell, first-fix, and final sign-off. You never pay for uncompleted work.

Can you manage structural steel and wall removals?

Yes. We handle structural engineer calculations, RSJ steel supply, hydraulic jacking, building inspector notifications, and building control completion certificates.

Are your building trades directly managed?

Yes. All tradesmen are directly managed by Simplicity, ensuring consistent daily momentum, spotless site conduct, and uncompromising standards from start to finish.
