Questions

Before you book

How do I get a price for my move?

Send us the basics through the enquiry form: the addresses or areas, your moving date and a rough description of the contents. We will come back with what we need to confirm a price.

Do you cover the whole of Birmingham?

Birmingham is our home patch. Tell us the postcodes at both ends and we will confirm whether the move works for us and what it would involve.

Can you pack for me?

Yes. We can pack everything ahead of the day, pack only the fragile and awkward items, or leave the packing to you and handle the carrying. Say which you prefer in the enquiry.

What about large or awkward items?

Wardrobes, pianos, American fridge-freezers and anything that will not fit through a normal doorway all need planning. Mention them when you enquire so we can size the job correctly.

How long does a house move take?

It depends on the size of the property, access at both ends and the distance. We will give you a realistic time expectation with the quote rather than an optimistic one on the day.
