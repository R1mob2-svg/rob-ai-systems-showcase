Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from homeowners considering roofing repairs or replacements.

How do I know if my roof needs a full replacement or just a repair?

If leaks are isolated to a broken tile, slipped flashing, or cracked mortar, a localized repair is often sufficient. However, if the felt underlay has rotted, battens are perishing, or tiles are porous and widespread leaks occur, a full reroofing provides long-term cost savings and structural security.

Do you provide free roof inspections and quotes?

Yes. Rooftopia Limited provides obligation-free on-site roof evaluations and transparent written estimates across Walsall and the West Midlands.

How long does a standard pitched roof replacement take?

A standard 3-bedroom semi-detached house reroof typically takes between 3 to 5 working days, weather permitting. We keep your property fully watertight and protected throughout every stage of the project.

Are your roofing works guaranteed?

Every major reroofing and flat roofing installation carries comprehensive workmanship and manufacturer material warranties for complete peace of mind.
