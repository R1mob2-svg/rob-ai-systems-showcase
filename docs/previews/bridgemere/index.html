Planning Your Project

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful questions to discuss before starting an extension, renovation or loft conversion.

Will my home extension need planning permission?

Some extensions qualify as permitted development; others need planning permission. The type of property, design and local restrictions all matter. Check the position with your local planning authority before work begins.

What approvals are needed for structural changes?

Structural alterations commonly require suitable design and Building Regulations approval. Ask who will prepare the calculations, coordinate Building Control inspections and supply the completion documentation.

How long does a typical building project take?

Timescales depend on the agreed scope, surveys, approvals, ground conditions, material availability and weather. Request a written programme with key milestones rather than relying on an unsupported fixed completion promise.

What should be included in a detailed quotation?

Look for a clear scope of work, exclusions, stage payments, insurance evidence, site access arrangements, waste removal, provisional sums and a process for approving any changes.
