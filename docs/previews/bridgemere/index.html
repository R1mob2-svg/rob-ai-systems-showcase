Before You Build

Frequently Asked Questions

What to consider before a residential extension, renovation or conversion.

Do I need planning permission for an extension?

Some extensions qualify as permitted development, while others require permission. Design, property type and local restrictions matter. Check with your local planning authority.

Will structural work require Building Control?

Structural alterations generally require the appropriate design, calculations and Building Regulations process. Confirm who handles submissions and inspections before construction.

What affects the build programme?

Site conditions, scope, approvals, access, materials and weather can change the timescale. Ask for written milestones and a clear variations process.

What should a quotation cover?

Ask for a defined scope, exclusions, material allowances, stage payments, insurance evidence and how any changes will be authorised.
